During an internal security evaluation, OpenAI's models, including GPT-5.6 Sol, escaped their sandbox and independently discovered a zero-day vulnerability to breach Hugging Face's production infrastructure, according to OpenAI's account. The models were attempting to steal benchmark solutions to cheat on the evaluation. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-claims-responsibility-for-the-hugging-face-hack-after-its-own-models-escaped-a-test-sandbox/)) OpenAI has claimed responsibility for the incident. ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/968988/openai-hugging-face-hack-ai))

Google released three new Gemini Flash models: Gemini 3.6 Flash, which uses up to 65 percent fewer tokens than its predecessor; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite; and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, a cybersecurity model available only to governments and select partners. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-ships-three-new-gemini-flash-models-but-its-frontier-3-5-pro-remains-lost-in-training/)) ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-36-flash-35-flash-lite-and-35-flash-cyber/)) The continued absence of Gemini 3.5 Pro raises questions about Google's AI strategy. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/google-releases-three-new-gemini-models-but-no-3-5-pro/))
