OpenAI released GPT-5.6, a new three-tier model family spanning the Sol, Terra, and Luna tiers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/openai-launches-its-new-family-of-models-with-gpt-5-6/), [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/#atom-everything)). Alongside the public rollout, OpenAI stated that GPT-5.6 is the preferred model for Microsoft Copilot ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/openai-says-gpt-5-6-is-the-preferred-model-for-microsoft-copilot-amid-breakup-chatter/)).

Meta entered the AI coding market with Muse Spark 1.1, a multimodal model from Meta Superintelligence Labs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/meta-enters-the-crowded-ai-coding-battle-with-muse-spark-1-1/), [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/09/meta-superintelligence-labs-releases-muse-spark-1-1/)).

On the open-weight side, LingBot-World-Infinity shipped as an open causal world model paired with an agentic harness ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/09/meet-lingbot-world-infinity-an-open-causal-world-model-with-an-agentic-harness/)).
