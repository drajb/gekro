Liquid AI released LFM2.5-24B-A2B, an open-weight 24B Mixture-of-Experts reasoning model with 2B active parameters designed for on-device agent deployment ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/14/liquid-ai-lfm2-5-24b-a2b)). The model achieved 48.09 on LiveClaw and decoded at 184 tokens per second on an Apple M5 Max, targeting developers building local reasoning systems ([Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/LiquidAI/lfm25-24b-a2b)).

Google made Gemini 4 Argon generally available on the Gemini API, AI Studio, and the Gemini app, with support for up to 1M output tokens ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/13/google-gemini-4-argon-api-release/)). The sparse MoE model leads the Vals Index at 68.90% ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/13/google-gemini-4-argon-api-release/)). Separately, Google DeepMind and GovTech Singapore demonstrated lightweight on-device Gemma models for conversational scam detection with zero cloud data egress ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/on-device-ai-scam-detection-mddi/)).
