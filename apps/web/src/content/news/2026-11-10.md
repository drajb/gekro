Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, offering the same underlying model with two distinct safeguard configurations. Fable 5.2 scores 49.1 percent on FrontierCode 2.0 and carries a 1M-token context window at pricing of $8/$40 per million tokens ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2-release/)). On the Remote Labor Index, Fable 5.2 completes 41.3 percent of real freelance jobs at professional quality, more than double the next-best systems Opus 5.5 and GPT 6 Astra, up from a top score of 2.5 percent a year ago ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropics-fable-5-2-completes-41-percent-of-real-freelance-jobs-up-from-2-5-percent-a-year-ago/)). Google DeepMind released AlphaProof Code, with agents writing three million lines of proofs in Lean to verify Signal's core encryption ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)).

Meta introduced Muse Ember, a small model designed for repetitive agent work including tool calls, routing, and data extraction, with a 1M-token context window. It ties Claude Haiku 5.5 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at roughly a third of the cost per task ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/09/meta-muse-ember-small-agent-model/)). Adobe launched agentic AI features across Photoshop, Premiere, and Firefly, including a new prototype builder tool in Firefly ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/adobe-adds-more-ai-to-its-products-and-launches-an-mcp/)).
