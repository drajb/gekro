Google DeepMind released Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking, two audio models designed for developers that ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-launches-gemini-3-8-live-to-take-on-openais-gpt-live-1-at-a-fraction-of-the-cost/)) top the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech leaderboard. The models are priced at $1.38 per hour of voice conversation, substantially undercutting OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 offering. The release represents a competitive pricing move in the real-time voice inference space as both vendor and open-weight approaches mature.

Meta launched a WhatsApp Business MCP server enabling developers to integrate AI coding agents like Claude, Cursor, Codex, and ChatGPT into WhatsApp Business workflow automation ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/meta-now-lets-ai-agents-handle-the-boring-parts-of-whatsapp-business-setup/)). The MCP integration allows agents to handle setup, messaging templates, testing, and troubleshooting tasks. The release demonstrates expanding tool availability for agent orchestration across enterprise messaging platforms.
