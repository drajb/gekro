Cerebras introduced its CS-4 AI accelerator, which the company characterizes as the fastest system in the industry ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/cerebras-unveils-cs-4-with-double-the-performance-on-the-same-chip)). The chip achieves double the performance of its predecessor while maintaining the same physical form factor, addressing inference and training workload efficiency for teams operating custom silicon stacks.

Alibaba released Wan3.0, a video generation model that produces clips up to 30 seconds long from text, images, and documents ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/alibabas-wan3-0-generates-ai-videos-up-to-30-seconds-long-from-text-images-and-documents)). A 30-second 1080p clip costs $6. Separately, Hugging Face published a workflow guide for Gradio, detailing integration of AI pipelines into interactive applications ([Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/gradio-workflow-guide)).
