NVIDIA released Alpamayo 2 Super, a 34B open-weight vision-language-action model for autonomous driving and robotaxis under the OpenMDW-1.1 permissive license, enabling fine-tuning, derivatives, and commercial redistribution ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/05/nvidia-alpamayo-2-super-open-vla-model-autonomous-driving/)). The model pairs a 32B Cosmos 3 Super Reasoner backbone with a 2.3B diffusion action decoder and achieved a score of 79.2 on the cited benchmark.

CopilotKit open-sourced the Channels SDK, an MIT-licensed library that runs existing AG-UI agents inside Slack and Microsoft Teams, shipping five platform adapters and a documented runtime contract in version 0.5.0 ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/04/copilotkit-open-sources-channels-sdk/)). Separately, Cursor Research published Mixture-of-Kittens (MoK), an open-source MoE training megakernel that fuses all mixture-of-experts communication and computation into a single deterministic kernel, achieving up to 2.37x speedup over the strongest public baseline on GB300 NVL72 racks ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/04/cursor-open-sources-mixture-of-kittens-mok-a-deterministic-moe-training-megakernel-for-gb300-nvl72-racks/)).
