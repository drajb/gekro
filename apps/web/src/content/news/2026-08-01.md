DeepSeek released an updated V4 Flash model on July 31 that jumped ten points to score 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it one point behind OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna while operating at roughly 60 percent lower cost per task ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/new-deepseek-flash-model-matches-openais-gpt-5-6-luna-at-roughly-60-percent-lower-cost/)).

Thinking Machines, the AI lab founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, released Inkling Small, an open-weights reasoning model less than one-third the size of its predecessor that outperforms the original on several coding and reasoning benchmarks ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/thinking-machines-bets-on-efficiency-over-size-with-its-second-model-inkling-small/)). DeepSeek V4 Flash also received a new release checkpoint on July 31 ([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/31/deepseek-v4-flash-0731/#atom-everything)).
