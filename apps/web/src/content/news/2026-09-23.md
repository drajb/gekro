Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, described as matching Claude Fable 5.1 performance on most tasks while reducing inference costs by approximately 40 percent compared to Opus 5 ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/claude-opus-5-5-matches-fable-5-1-at-40-percent-lower-cost-as-anthropic-promises-to-fix-claudish-writing)). Independent benchmarking placed Opus 5.5 ahead of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra on most tasks despite the cost reduction ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/claude-opus-5-5-matches-fable-5-1-at-40-percent-lower-cost-as-anthropic-promises-to-fix-claudish-writing)).

OpenAI simultaneously launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna, two models that cut token pricing in half relative to previous generations while maintaining predecessor performance levels ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openais-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-cut-prices-in-half-but-barely-move-the-needle-on-performance)). Third-party analysis found minimal gains in raw intelligence metrics from the new OpenAI models ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openais-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-cut-prices-in-half-but-barely-move-the-needle-on-performance)). Both releases mark a shift toward token economics optimization rather than capability expansion.
