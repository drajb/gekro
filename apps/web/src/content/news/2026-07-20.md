Feyn Labs has released SQRL, a family of text-to-SQL models designed for self-hosted inference that inspect a database using read-only probes before committing to a query. The flagship SQRL-35B-A3B reports 70.6% execution accuracy on BIRD Dev and distills into self-hostable 4B and 9B checkpoints ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/19/feyn-ai-releases-sqrl-a-text-to-sql-model-family-that-inspects-the-database-before-writing-a-query/)).

Moonshot has temporarily paused new Kimi K3 subscriptions after demand maxed out GPU capacity within 48 hours of launch, with the company planning to split its subscription model to distribute computing resources more evenly ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/moonshot-pauses-new-kimi-k3-subscriptions-after-gpu-demand-maxes-out-in-48-hours/)). In related developments, a community developer fine-tuned OpenBMB's MiniCPM5-1B on Claude Fable 5 traces to produce a 657MB local reasoning model with 128K context ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/19/someone-fine-tuned-openbmbs-minicpm5-1b-on-claude-fable-5-traces-to-ship-a-657mb-local-thinking-model/)), and Perplexity AI released WANDR, an open benchmark with 500 evidence-heavy tasks for evaluating research agents ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/19/perplexity-ai-releases-wandr-an-open-benchmark-evaluating-research-agents-that-must-search-wide-and-deep/)).
