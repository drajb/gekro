Liquid AI released two open-weight bidirectional encoders, LFM2.5-Encoder-230M and LFM2.5-Encoder-350M, built on the LFM2 hybrid backbone with 8,192-token context windows. ([Liquid AI](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/29/liquid-ai-releases-lfm2-5-encoder-230m-and-lfm2-5-encoder-350m-bidirectional-encoders-that-stay-fast-at-8k-context-on-cpu/)) The 350M model ranked fourth among 14 models on a 17-task benchmark suite covering GLUE, SuperGLUE, and multilingual evaluations, trailing only larger models. ([Liquid AI](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/29/liquid-ai-releases-lfm2-5-encoder-230m-and-lfm2-5-encoder-350m-bidirectional-encoders-that-stay-fast-at-8k-context-on-cpu/))

Fireworks AI released Fireworks Nexus, a routing and cost-control layer designed to integrate with existing developer tooling and direct routine coding work to open-weight models. ([Fireworks AI](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/28/fireworks-ai-releases-fireworks-nexus-a-drop-in-routing-and-cost-control-layer-that-moves-routine-coding-work-to-open-weight-models/)) The platform targets engineering organizations seeking to reduce API costs for repetitive tasks. ([Fireworks AI](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/28/fireworks-ai-releases-fireworks-nexus-a-drop-in-routing-and-cost-control-layer-that-moves-routine-coding-work-to-open-weight-models/))
