Together AI released Together Compiler, an agent-driven optimization loop that monitors inference traffic and re-compiles the forward pass in real time to match shifting patterns ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/05/together-ai-together-compiler-continual-compilation-ai-inference/)). In testing across multiple models, the system outperformed the best existing InferenceX configurations by up to 2.6x tokens per second within 24 hours per model, addressing a core cost vector for inference-heavy deployments.

Box announced an agent-first content retrieval system powered by Nvidia's Nemotron, claiming four times faster query response, 20% fewer errors, and 90% fewer tokens consumed compared to prior approaches ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/box-taps-nvidias-nemotron-for-new-agent-first-content-retrieval-system/)). Separately, Ricursive Intelligence released Ripple-65B Preview, a 65B open-weight dense model scoring 80.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and outperforming much larger open models ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/04/ricursive-ripple-65b-preview-open-weight-model/)), and webAI released TwIL-8B, an 8.8B reasoning model post-trained from IBM Granite that scored 98.2 on MATH-500 ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/04/webai-twil-8b-reasoning-model/)).
