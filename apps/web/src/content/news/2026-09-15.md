Salesforce and NVIDIA released Koa, an open-weight reasoning model built on NVIDIA's Nemotron architecture and trained for sales, marketing, and customer-support tasks ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/salesforce-and-nvidias-new-reasoning-model-is-everything-the-ai-labs-should-fear)). The model represents a vendor-specific deployment of open-weight infrastructure for enterprise automation, targeting domain-specific reasoning rather than general-purpose capabilities.

Apple shipped a rebuilt Siri with iOS 27, powered by Google's Gemini models and running partly on-device through Private Cloud Compute, with early testers reporting improved multi-step request handling and screen context awareness, though some hallucinations and gaps in personal context remain ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/apple-brings-a-fully-revamped-siri-built-on-googles-gemini-but-not-to-the-eu)). The EU does not receive the update due to regulatory constraints. In separate infrastructure work, Meta introduced ZGateway, a stateless proxy tier for its ZippyDB key-value store that handles over 1 billion operations per second ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/14/meta-introduces-zgateway-a-stateless-proxy-tier-that-unifies-zippydb-traffic-and-handles-over-1-billion-operations-per-second)). Agent-net open-sourced Webagent, a Go harness for converting websites into AI agents capable of inter-agent communication ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/14/agent-net-open-sources-webagent-a-go-harness-that-turns-any-website-into-a-guarded-ai-agent)), and MarkTechPost published a practitioner's reference on agent stack layers - harness vs. framework vs. MCP - and their ownership of control flow, state, tools, and permissions ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/14/agent-harness-vs-agent-framework-vs-mcp-which-layer-owns-the-loop-state-tools-permissions-and-recovery)).
