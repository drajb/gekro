Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open-weights speech language model with 30B parameters and 4.3B active parameters. The model transcribes 40 languages, adds speaker labels and word timestamps, and achieved a 4.71 percent mean WER on the English Open ASR Leaderboard ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/28/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-transcribe-open-speech-llm)).

IBM released Granite Switch 4.1.1, an approach that trains an external instruction layer on task feedback while leaving the base model and harness unchanged, enabling model updates without full retraining ([Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-switch-4-1-1)). Separately, Block's AI lab released Dessa, a full-duplex speech-to-speech model trained only on human conversation audio that runs locally on a MacBook Pro M4 Max, though Block has published no model weights or detailed benchmarks ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/29/block-dessa-full-duplex-speech-model)).
