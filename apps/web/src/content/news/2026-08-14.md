Google released Gemini 3.7 Flash, arriving just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash with claimed improvements in coding and agentic workloads. According to Google's benchmarks, the model outperforms Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.6 Terra while pricing at 50 percent below its predecessor, positioning it as a cost-efficient option for agent deployment and development tasks ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gemini-3-7-flash-lands-with-coding-gains-and-undercuts-its-three-week-old-predecessors-price-by-50/)). Separately, DeepSeek moved its V4-Pro model out of testing and released Harness v0.1, its agent orchestration software, under the MIT open-source license ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/deepseek-launches-an-improved-v4-pro-model-raises-api-prices-and-makes-its-agent-software-open-source/)). DeepSeek also raised API pricing, with cache hits increasing to six times their current cost, which affects cost economics for agents that repeatedly access the same data.

Writer introduced a new model built as a post-training variant on Z.ai's open-source GLM-5.2, emphasizing lower token costs and deployment-ready capabilities as a cost-containment strategy ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/writer-introduces-new-ai-model-and-upgraded-harness-to-contain-token-costs/)). OpenAI launched Ultrafast, a preview mode for GPT-5.6 Sol that achieves 14x speedup, targeting enterprise users concerned with inference latency ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/openai-introduces-ultrafast-a-new-mode-that-makes-gpt-5-6-sol-work-at-14x-the-speed/)).
