The US Commerce Department authorized Anthropic to distribute Claude Mythos 5 to a group of roughly 100 companies and federal agencies on June 27, partially lifting the export-control block imposed on June 12 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html), [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/26/tech/anthropic-mythos-release)). Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick signed a formal letter stating that appropriate safeguards were in place following negotiations between Anthropic and government officials ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/trump-admin-releases-anthropic-mythos-to-be-used-by-more-than-100-us-companies-agencies/)). The authorized pool covers members of Anthropic's Project Glasswing - a vetted security-research program spanning many Fortune 500 firms - and the letter waives standard export-license requirements for foreign-national employees at those institutions ([Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/us-government-gives-anthropic-green-light-limited-re-release-mythos-5-rcna352018)). The June 12 directive had suspended all access after officials assessed that Mythos 5's cybersecurity capabilities could accelerate vulnerability exploitation by adversarial actors ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/12/anthropic-trump-mythos-fable-national-security)). Claude Fable 5 remains suspended under the original order, with no published timeline for broader re-release ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5943549-anthropic-mythos-5-access/)).

OpenAI reached general availability for Codex Remote on all paid ChatGPT plans on June 27 - covering Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Education subscribers - giving users phone-based control of long-running coding sessions on connected Mac or Windows hosts ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319201/20260627/openai-codex-remote-goes-live-all-plans-phone-control-now-secured-qr-relay.htm)). The GA build replaces the previous shared-secret connection model with one-to-one QR-code pairing that enforces account matching and any configured MFA or passkey steps before granting phone access. A new DigitalOcean Droplet Workspace plugin lets Codex provision a cloud VM, configure SSH, and register it as a remote workspace from inside the app.

OpenAI also completed the retirement of GPT-4.5 from ChatGPT on June 26, concluding a 30-day sunset announced May 28 ([OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)). Existing conversations previously tied to GPT-4.5 now route to GPT-5.5, and custom GPTs were migrated automatically. The model remains available via the API without change, preserving backward compatibility for developers running it outside the chat interface.
