Nvidia has open-sourced NVIDIA Kubernetes Engine (NVKE) and NVIDIA Kubernetes Recipes (NVKR) under Apache 2.0 license, both in public preview. The tools enable AI factories to run managed Kubernetes services on their own bare-metal GPU servers, addressing infrastructure deployment complexity for teams operating self-hosted AI clusters ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-nvke-nvkr-managed-kubernetes-gpu/)).

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra, omnimodal decision models capable of processing text, images, and audio and returning calibrated probabilities with zero output tokens. The d1.2-ultra variant scored 75 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1 ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/17/liquid-ai-d1-2-omnimodal-decision-models/)). Separately, Sarvam AI launched Sarvam Token Factory, an India-hosted inference platform serving eight open models including Sarvam 105B, GLM 5.3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and Gemma 4 31B through an OpenAI-compatible API ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/17/sarvam-token-factory-india-inference-platform/)).
