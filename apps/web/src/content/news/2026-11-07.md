Buoyant has released Kinference, an Apache 2.0 inference control plane for vLLM on Kubernetes that adds declarative routing policies, KV-cache-aware load balancing, and per-tenant metrics. In Buoyant's own benchmark, the system cut median time-to-first-token by 95 percent. ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/07/buoyant-kinference-open-source-inference-control-plane-kubernetes-vllm/))

Recursive Intelligence is releasing Ripple-65B Preview, a 65-billion-parameter open-weight model fully licensed under Apache 2.0 that achieves the top score among open-source U.S. models on TerminalBench 2.1 for agentic coding at a total R&D cost of $32 million. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/ripple-65b-preview-delivers-strong-agentic-coding-results-as-a-fully-apache-2-0-open-us-model/)) Separately, Nous Research retrained Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-27B into Arete-27B using 53 million tokens of supervised fine-tuning, reducing the model's propaganda rate from 22 percent to less than one percent while preserving general capabilities. ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/state-propaganda-in-some-chinese-ai-models-is-only-a-thin-layer-that-fine-tuning-can-strip-away/))
